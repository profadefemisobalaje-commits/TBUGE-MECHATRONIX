*Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Volume Five: Natural Language Processing*

First edition. TBUGE Mechatronix.

This volume treats language as evidence rather than magic. It counts words before it embeds them, builds a language model small enough to compute on paper, and measures every claim on text the model has not seen.

The programme runs to forty-eight volumes. Volume Four gave the machine eyes; this one gives it the written word and the means to be held to account for what it says about it.

Course codes have been left out on purpose. A subject should be named, not numbered.
