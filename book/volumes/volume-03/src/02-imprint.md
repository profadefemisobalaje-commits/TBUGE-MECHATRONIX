*Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Volume Three: Machine Learning*

First edition. TBUGE Mechatronix.

This volume assumes the arithmetic of secondary school and the ideas of Volume One. It teaches the methods with a pencil first and a computer second, on the principle that a model you cannot compute by hand is a model you cannot debug.

The programme runs to forty-eight volumes. Volume One asked what intelligence is; Volume Two built the machine. This one gives that machine the ability to improve from examples.

Course codes have been left out on purpose. A subject should be named, not numbered.
