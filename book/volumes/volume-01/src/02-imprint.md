*Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Volume One: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence*

First edition. TBUGE Mechatronix.

This volume stands alone. It assumes no previous study of computing, no mathematics beyond secondary school, and no equipment beyond a pen, some paper and a calculator. The volumes that follow take up the ideas introduced here and build a working knowledge of robotics, machine learning, control, perception, planning and the ethics that bind them.

The series runs to forty-eight volumes, one for each course in the programme. They are designed to be read in order, but each is written so that a reader who comes to it late can still follow the argument.

Course codes have been left out on purpose. A subject should be named, not numbered.
