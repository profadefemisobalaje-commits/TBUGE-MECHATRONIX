# Sources and Further Reading

The factual claims in this volume are drawn from the works below, and from the standards named in the chapter on safety. Component figures are typical values from manufacturers' datasheets; where a number is an illustrative example, the text says so.

## Books on robot mechanics

Craig, J. *Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control*. The standard treatment of frames, kinematics and Jacobians; Chapter 1 to 3 pair well with this volume.

Siciliano, B., and Khatib, O., eds. *Springer Handbook of Robotics*. Comprehensive, expensive, and the right place to look up a mechanism you have not met before.

Norton, R. *Design of Machinery*. For linkages, cams and four-bars, with the degree-of-freedom counting done properly.

Shigley, J., and Mischke, C. *Mechanical Engineering Design*. The source for deflection, fatigue and shaft design when a robot link must survive ten million cycles.

## Motors, drives and power

Hughes, A., and Drury, B. *Electric Motors and Drives*. Clear on the torque-speed relationship and on what a motor's ratings actually mean.

Krishnan, R. *Electric Motor Drives: Modeling, Analysis and Control*. For readers who want the electronics and the control together.

Linden, D., and Reddy, T., eds. *Handbook of Batteries*. The reference on chemistry, capacity and safety.

## Electronics and embedded systems

Horowitz, P., and Hill, W. *The Art of Electronics*. The book people keep on the bench, for the chapters on noise, grounding and power supplies.

White, E. *Making Embedded Systems*. Practical guidance on interrupts, timing and the discipline of small systems.

## Safety and standards

ISO 10218-1 and ISO 10218-2. *Robots and robotic devices — Safety requirements for industrial robots.*

ISO/TS 15066. *Robots and robotic devices — Collaborative robots.*

ISO 13849-1. *Safety of machinery — Safety-related parts of control systems.*

IEC 61508. *Functional safety of electrical/electronic/programmable electronic safety-related systems.*

Occupational Safety and Health Administration. Investigations and publications on robot-related fatalities, including the 1979 Ford case.

## Robotics in practice

Brooks, R. *Cambrian Intelligence*. The collected papers on subsumption, still the clearest statement of behaviour-based robotics.

Thrun, S., Burgard, W., and Fox, D. *Probabilistic Robotics*. For the localisation and mapping that this volume deliberately leaves out.

Wurman, P., D'Andrea, R., and Mountz, M. (2008). “Coordinating Hundreds of Cooperative, Autonomous Vehicles in Warehouses.” *AI Magazine*. The Kiva system described by the people who built it.

---

*Every calculation in this volume can be reproduced with a calculator. Where a component's rating is quoted, it is a typical figure for that class of part rather than a specification of any particular product.*
