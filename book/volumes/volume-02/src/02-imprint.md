*Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Volume Two: Robotics Fundamentals*

First edition. TBUGE Mechatronix.

This volume assumes the vocabulary of Volume One: agents, sensors, actuators, policies and performance measures. It assumes nothing else. No electronics background is needed, no workshop is required, and every calculation can be done with a pencil and a calculator.

The series runs to forty-eight volumes, one for each course in the programme. Volume One asked what intelligence means in a machine. This one builds the machine that could have it.

Course codes have been left out on purpose. A subject should be named, not numbered.
