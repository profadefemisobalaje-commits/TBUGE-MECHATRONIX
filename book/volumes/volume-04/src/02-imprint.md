*Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Volume Four: Computer Vision*

First edition. TBUGE Mechatronix.

This volume assumes that a camera is a sensor and that a picture is a measurement. It teaches vision from the pixel upwards: how light becomes numbers, how those numbers are filtered, how structure is found in them, and how a system is built that keeps working when the light changes.

The programme runs to forty-eight volumes. Volume Three taught a machine to improve from examples; this one gives it eyes, and the judgement to know when its eyes are lying.

Course codes have been left out on purpose. A subject should be named, not numbered.
